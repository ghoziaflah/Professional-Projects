# Professional Projects

A collection of professional projects focused on **data analytics, business intelligence, and data-driven problem solving** across different industries.

This repository showcases practical experience in translating business needs into analytical solutions, developing interactive dashboards, and generating insights to support operational and business decision-making.

## Projects

### 1. Class Availability Dashboard for Education Services

**Industry:** Education  
**Tools:** Looker Studio, Google Sheets

Developed an interactive dashboard to improve class availability visibility and support enrollment coordination across 10 learning centers. The dashboard enables Education Consultants to explore available classes based on learning center, program, level, and schedule.

🔗 [View Project](./Class_Availability_Dashboard_for_Education_Services/)

### 2. Passenger Revenue & Volume Monitoring Dashboard for Railway Operations

**Industry:** Transportation  
**Tools:** Power BI, Microsoft Excel, Python

Developed an interactive dashboard to monitor passenger transportation performance, covering revenue realization, passenger volume, daily revenue, and train occupancy across stations and service classes.

🔗 [View Project](./Passenger_Revenue_and_Volume_Monitoring_Dashboard_for_Railway_Operations/)
